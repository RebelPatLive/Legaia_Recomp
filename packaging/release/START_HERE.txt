Legaia Recomp @VERSION@

Launch Legaia Recomp on Windows, or the AppImage on Linux, and choose your
legally owned Legend of Legaia disc when prompted. No game disc or retail BIOS
image is included. OpenBIOS is bundled.

Tip: the "game" folder next to the program is a handy place to keep your
disc image (.cue and .bin files). The launcher still asks you to pick the .cue
file, so it can live anywhere; this folder just keeps it tidy.

Use the launcher to configure controls and mods. Internal Resolution starts
at 4x on a fresh profile; its 1x through 32x choices remain subject to your
GPU's texture and memory limits. Existing saved choices remain yours.

Keep your native memory cards and settings when updating the application.
